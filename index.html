Preprints

Causal Kernel Descent for Finite-Order Linear Recurrences

Hao Zhang · Preprint, 2026 · PDF

We construct a counterexample to prediction consistency of unnormalized causal kernel descent (CKD) after state augmentation, and prove consistency of source-normalized CKD for bounded finite-order linear recurrences.

We characterize a nontrivial family of two-lag linear systems preserving a shared invariant metric, and construct a CKD attention realization for this family.

Log-Free Hyperelliptic Specialization and Class Groups of Quadratic Fields

Hao Zhang and Zheyuan Jin · Preprint, 2026 · PDF

We prove that there are ≫ X1/3 imaginary quadratic fields K with |Disc K| ≤ X and class-group 5-rank at least two. This removes the logarithmic loss from the previous lower bound of Bartz, Levin, and Thamminana, ≫ X1/3 / (log X)2.

More generally, we refine the quantitative specialization theorem of Kulkarni and Levin for hyperelliptic curves over ℚ whose degree-two map has a rational ramification point and whose branch-point Galois orbits each have size at most six. The orbit-size condition is automatic in genus one and two.
